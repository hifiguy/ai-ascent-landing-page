## 🎯 Target Profiles — Who to DM

### Tier 1: Warm Contacts (Highest Priority — DM First)
People who already know you or have interacted with your content.

| # | Profile Type | Where to Find | Why They Convert |
|---|-------------|---------------|------------------|
| 1-5 | **Current LinkedIn connections** in marketing roles | Your LinkedIn connections list, filter by "Marketing" | They trust you already |
| 6-8 | **People who liked/commented** on your recent posts | LinkedIn notifications, last 30 days | Already engaged with you |
| 9-10 | **Former colleagues** in marketing/tech | LinkedIn connections | Personal relationship |

### Tier 2: Warm-Adjacent (They Posted About AI)
People who recently posted about AI, marketing tools, or productivity.

| # | Profile Type | Where to Find | Search Terms |
|---|-------------|---------------|-------------|
| 11-14 | **Marketing managers** posting about AI | LinkedIn search: "AI marketing" → Posts tab | "AI tools", "ChatGPT for marketing", "AI prompts" |
| 15-17 | **Content marketers** discussing productivity | Twitter/X search | "AI content creation", "marketing automation" |
| 18-20 | **SME owners** exploring AI | LinkedIn search: filter by company size 50-500 | "AI for business", "generative AI" |

### Tier 3: Cold Outreach (Newsletter/Community Owners)
| # | Profile Type | Where to Find |
|---|-------------|---------------|
| 21-25 | Marketing newsletter operators | Substack, Beehiiv trending in marketing |
| 26-28 | AI tool reviewers / YouTubers | YouTube search "AI marketing tools" |

## 📝 Ready-to-Send DM Templates

### Template 1: LinkedIn DM (Warm — They Posted About AI)

**When to use:** They recently posted or commented about AI, marketing tools, or productivity.

```
Hey [First Name] — saw your post about [specific topic they posted about].
Really resonated with me, especially the part about [specific detail].

I actually just put together a cheat sheet of 50 AI prompts specifically
for marketers — organized by function (content, ads, SEO, email, etc.).
Each one is copy-paste ready.

Thought it might be useful given what you shared. Want me to send
you the link? Totally free, no catch.
```

**If they say yes, send:**
```
Awesome! Here you go:
https://hifiguy.github.io/ai-ascent-landing-page/?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=dm&utm_campaign=launch_day1

Quick tip: Start with prompt #7 (Content Repurposing Engine) —
it's the one most people tell me saves them the most time.

Let me know what you think! 🙌
```

**Follow-up (if no response after 48 hours):**
```
Hey [First Name] — just bumping this in case it got buried.
No worries if it's not your thing! Just thought the [content/ads/SEO]
prompts might save you some time based on your work at [Company]. 🙂
```

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Personalize every message** — mention their specific post, company, or role
2. **Don't lead with the link** on LinkedIn — ask if they want it first
3. **DO include the link** on Twitter — DMs are more casual
4. **Best times to DM:** 8-10 AM and 1-3 PM in recipient's timezone
5. **Track everything** — use the tracking sheet above
6. **Follow up once** after 48 hours — then stop (don't be pushy)
7. **Respond within 5 minutes** when someone replies (speed = conversion)
