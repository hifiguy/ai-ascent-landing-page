## 🔍 How to Find Your 20 Warm LinkedIn Contacts RIGHT NOW

### Step 1: Search Your Existing Connections (5 min)
1. Go to LinkedIn → My Network → Connections
2. Search: "marketing manager" — note first 5 results
3. Search: "content marketing" — note next 3 results
4. Search: "digital marketing" — note next 2 results

### Step 2: Check Recent Engagers (5 min)
1. Go to your recent LinkedIn posts
2. Click on likes/comments
3. Note anyone in marketing roles — these are your warmest leads

### Step 3: Search for AI Posters (10 min)
1. LinkedIn search bar → Posts tab
2. Search: "AI marketing tools" — filter to past week
3. Find 5 people who posted about AI in marketing
4. Check their profile — ideal: marketing manager at company with 50-500 employees
5. Note their name, company, and what they posted about (for personalization)

### Step 4: Former Colleagues (2 min)
1. Think of 2-3 former colleagues in marketing/tech
2. Add them to your list

**Total time: ~22 minutes to build your full list of 20 contacts**
